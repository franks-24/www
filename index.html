04 / THE DETAILS

Everything you need to know before you make your next move.

What is Franks24?+

Franks24 is the main brand and home for its services. Dispatch is one of those services, focused on connecting people and work.

What is Dispatch?+

Dispatch by Franks24 brings job seekers and hiring teams into a shared platform for onboarding, opportunities and work coordination.

How do I access Dispatch?+

Visit dispatch.franks24.com. The platform is designed around Google sign-in. Authentication availability depends on its Firebase deployment configuration.

Can I use this website on my phone?+

Yes. Franks24 is designed for mobile, tablet and desktop, with responsive controls, keyboard support and reduced-motion accessibility.
